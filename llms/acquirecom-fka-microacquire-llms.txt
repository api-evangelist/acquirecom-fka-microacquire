# Acquire.com (fka MicroAcquire)

> Acquire.com (formerly MicroAcquire) is an online marketplace for buying and selling startups and online businesses, connecting vetted buyers with founders who want to sell. It offers deal discovery, financial metrics, escrow and closing tooling, buyer memberships, and M&A education via the Acquire Academy. Founded by Andrew Gazdecki and backed by Bessemer Venture Partners. As of this profile the company publishes no public developer API, SDKs, or webhook surface.
